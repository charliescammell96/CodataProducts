Do I need to know Excel or Power BI?

Not to understand the reports. The locally maintained Excel product requires basic spreadsheet use to add new data. The future Generator is designed to handle the report creation online.

What kind of data do I need?

It depends on the report. Sales Performance typically needs transaction dates, products, quantities and prices. Each product page will explain its required fields before you buy.

Can I keep using a report after I buy it?

Yes. The planned Excel product is a one-off purchase that you can keep using locally, adding or replacing source data in its dedicated data tab.

Is my business data stored on the website?

The public demonstration is entirely fictional and doesn't receive your files. A production upload service will have its own security, retention, privacy and processing terms before it goes live.

Do I have to book a call to buy something?

No. CoData is being designed for self-service discovery and purchasing. The storefront is currently a preview, so live checkout will only be enabled once the first products and payment flow are ready.
